## Human inspection points I1–I5

*July 2026 · Amends §3 (milestones), §6 (documentation register), §12 (readiness gates). Adds a new §3a. Companion to DN-5 and the Stage-1 test plan.*

---

## A1.1 Why

The validation battery is numeric end to end. Between the Stage-1 believability test and the public beta there is no scheduled moment at which a human looks at what the system produces.

This is a real gap and not a procedural one. **Cont's stylised facts are necessary, not sufficient.** A generator can sit inside every pre-registered band — tail index, ACF of |r| profile, aggregational Gaussianity, discriminative-score ceiling — and still produce a decade that no allocator recognises: regime durations too short, transitions that resolve in three months, ten years containing no episode. None of that is in the panel; all of it is obvious on sight to someone who has allocated through the real ones.

The amendment adds five inspection points, each attached to a step that already exists, and one small build item that makes them repeatable rather than one-off.

**Standing rule:** an inspection point is *diagnostic with teeth*, not a gate. It cannot pass a model the battery failed. It can, and must, generate mandatory work items — recorded against the relevant D- or SM- decision — before the associated gate closes.

## A1.3 Protocols

### I1 · De-smoothing inspection *(do this week)*

Per sleeve: reported and de-smoothed series overlaid; fitted MA(2) or AR(1) weights; ACF(1–6) before and after; ratio of de-smoothed to reported volatility; ratio of de-smoothed to reported equity beta.

**Red flags** — each is a work item against D1, not a curiosity:
- True vol exceeding ~2.5× reported for any sleeve. Venture is the expected offender and the least reliable correction in the panel
- Negative or oscillating MA weights, implying a correction that overshoots and reverses
- Post-correction ACF materially negative — over-differencing, not de-smoothing
- Any sleeve where the correction moves the *mean*. Geltner and GLM both leave means broadly intact; a shifted mean means the estimator is misspecified

### I2 · Blinded decade discrimination *(the important one)*

The human analogue of the discriminative score, and for a practitioner audience considerably more persuasive than a classifier AUC.

**Stimuli.** ~40 items, roughly half real. Real decades are overlapping ten-year windows drawn from the JST and factor history; synthetic decades are drawn from the candidate ensemble. Selection by seed, not by hand.

**Rendering is the main threat to validity.** Real history leaks through its artifacts unless suppressed: strip all dates from axes, normalise every series to 100 at start, hold frequency and variable set identical across arms, apply identical display smoothing and identical axis ranges. If a rater can identify the real arm from the chrome rather than the dynamics, the study measures nothing.

**Response per item:** real or synthetic · confidence 1–5 · **free text: what gave it away**.

**The free text is the payload.** The accuracy number is a headline; the reasons are the work items. "Vol clusters resolve too quickly," "no episode here," "rates and credit move together too cleanly" each map to a specific layer and become a change against DN-1 or a robustness axis.

**Be honest about power.** With ~40 items and a handful of raters the confidence interval on accuracy is wide, which is exactly why this is not a kill gate. Report the interval; do not report a p-value as though it settled something.

**Conflict control.** You are both builder and rater. The blind set is generated by seed, the rater does not choose it, and the answer key is written to a file whose **hash is committed to the repo before the viewing session**. Cheap, and it converts the exercise from an anecdote into an auditable artifact.

**Independent reviewer (W11) attends.** Their presence at a pre-seal exercise is part of the effective-challenge record, on the same logic as the pre-registration workshop.

**Publication.** A practitioner discrimination study is a natural component of TERRARIUM-Bench and gives the working paper a result that is not another battery table. Design it to publication standard now; the marginal cost is the pre-committed key and a written protocol.

### I3 · Mapping inspection

Each sleeve's path overlaid on its principal factor driver, plus a **cross-correlogram at lags −6 to +6 months**.

This is the SM-3 double-lag test rendered visually, and it is the highest diagnostic yield per minute in the register. Expected: de-smoothed HF sleeves peak at lag 0; reported private sleeves peak at the kernel lag. **A de-smoothed private sleeve peaking at lag 2 means the lag has been counted twice** — estimated on lagged betas *and* re-applied by the kernel. The pending unit test from WP2.1b asserts this; the correlogram is how you see it when the test's tolerance is wrong.

Also check: buyout beta above 1.0 and below ~1.5; direct-lending duration near zero; every sign against the SM-4 constraint table.

### I4 · Toggle inspection

Reported and true planes side by side across a drawdown episode, 2021–2023 as the reference case. Three things must be visible:

1. Reported drawdown materially shallower than true
2. The gap **widening into the trough** — this is SM-11 state-dependent stickiness working, and its absence means the parameter is doing nothing
3. Private weight rising in the reported plane while falling in the true plane — the denominator effect, which is the D7 acceptance criterion and the flagship narrative of two launch worlds

If (2) and (3) do not appear here, they will not appear in the product, and the 2022 worlds ship without their own mechanic.

### I5 · First-run observation

Twenty real users against the §7.2 timings. Instrumented per sharing spec §12, but watched as well as measured — completion friction is the ⚑ open decision on default world length, and it is answered by observation rather than analytics.

## A1.4 Build item — the inspect renderer

These should not be five notebooks that rot.

**WP2R.4 — `--inspect`.** One code path that renders any RunRecord to a static HTML page: factor panel, sleeve panel, reported-vs-true toggle, episode annotations, correlogram grid. Reads the RunRecord only; no separate state.

Justification beyond inspection: it is roughly 80% of the M4 methodology-note figures and most of the walkthrough video source, so it is paid for twice before it is used once. Blind-set generation for I2 is a flag on the same path.
