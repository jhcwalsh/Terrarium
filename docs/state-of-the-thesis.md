# State of the thesis

**Date:** 2026-08-15 · **For:** the owner · **Written because:** trust in the approach
is wavering, and the right response is a decision made from the record — with success
defined forward, not recited backward.

---

## The thesis had two parts

1. **Generate** convincing counterfactual decades — the alternate-histories ambition.
2. **Exercise** an institutional portfolio against them — the twin, coverage,
   commitments, decision alpha.

Part 2 works. Part 1 has failed its own sealed criteria three times. The product
currently ships part 2 wearing part 1's clothes, and the disclosures that reconcile
them live in evidence files the player never sees. That gap — not any single result —
is where trust broke.

## The record, compressed

- **Proven and standing:** deterministic, audited rails end to end; declared-severity
  stress worlds whose severity binds (−37.5% decade at 6-month blocks, measured);
  coverage as *the* risk variable (E1 grid: worst coverage 0.10 → 1.57 as private
  allocation runs 15% → 55%); the forced secondary as the over-committed book's event;
  server-authoritative scoring; the behavioral result (the owner's own played decade:
  −2.33 alpha to timing moves that felt right).
- **Failed, three times, against sealed criteria:** learned generators versus the
  stitched-real-months benchmark. Campaign 2 promoted `hier-flow-v1` with recorded
  caveats; campaign 3 ended SHIP-BENCHMARK. The benchmark has never lost.
- **`hier-flow-v1`'s recorded gaps:** drawdowns understated ~2×, regime persistence
  undercalled, the battery's decade tier 73% structurally unavailable
  (`G2-EVIDENCE.md` §7–8). It makes decades that cohere and cannot hurt you.
- **Structural constraints:** the one-shot holdout is spent (WP5.6). Step 3's
  G1-completion is an honest FAIL. Nothing built on `hier-flow-v1` is decision-ready.
- **What a stress world is:** real at 6-month scale (joint history), constrained at
  the seams (nine level factors in tolerance; CPI's era is not), authored at decade
  scale (severity strata are declared — D-SC-1).

Everything above is in sealed evidence or the registers. The system has not told a
comfortable lie; that discipline is the asset this decision spends from.

---

## What success looks like

Three paths. Each gets a definition of success you could check from outside the
building — no appeals to effort, novelty, or the quality of our caveats.

### Path A — the trainer: prescribed decades, honestly worn

The product is a decision-discipline and liquidity-stress trainer. The decade is
declared, the months are real, and the product says so **on its face**.

Success is all of:

1. **In-surface honesty.** A first-time player learns from the play surface itself —
   not from evidence files — that the decade is prescribed and the months are real.
   The world card carries its precedent line and a "how this world was made"
   disclosure. *Test: after one session, a new player answers "did this decade
   happen?" correctly.*
2. **Severity binds, visibly.** Every scored world ships with its measured severity
   band on the hold-course book (the stress-03 measurement, repeated per world), and
   the band is shown where the player picks the world.
3. **The machinery can hurt you.** Over-committed configurations reach coverage
   breach and forced secondaries in play — the commitment lever (E1) is a playable
   control, so the sizing lesson is *earnable*, not narrated at the player.
4. **The lesson is measured, not asserted.** Across ranked sessions, decision-alpha
   distributions improve over a player's first N runs, and the Reckoning surfaces
   attribute the gap (timing vs. sizing) per decision.
5. **Nothing unscorable scores.** Leaderboard rows come only from worlds with
   declared, measured severity. Exploration worlds are visibly unranked.

**The bar to call it:** one external playtest cohort — even n=5 — completes ranked
sessions, and each player can restate unprompted what was real and what was declared.
Without any of us in the room.

### Path B — generated decades: the ambition, given a real bar

Success is a generator that passes **all** of the following, with thresholds and
judging code sealed before training begins (the existing pre-registration machinery
does this):

1. **Calibrated pain.** Generated decade drawdown distributions understate the
   historical reference by ≤ 1.25× (today: ~2×).
2. **Persistence.** Regime sojourn-time distributions inside sealed bands
   (today: undercalled).
3. **Judgeable.** Battery decade tier ≥ 90% structurally available (today: 27%).
4. **It can hurt a book without being told to.** The same over-committed grid that
   breaches under the stress compiler breaches comparably under the generator —
   severity that *emerges* rather than severity that is declared.
5. **Internal causality.** The generated policy rate responds to the world's own
   inflation and growth with the right sign and a plausible lag — a
   reaction-function test. This is the test that separates a generated decade from
   upholstery: the stitched worlds can never pass it, and nothing we have today does.
6. **Still beats the benchmark** on the sealed generation criterion. The conjunction
   of 1–6 has never been achieved. No appeal to the spent holdout is possible;
   evaluation uses newly pre-registered data or criteria only.

**Spending rule.** No campaign 4 without a new architectural idea aimed specifically
at 1–3, demonstrated first on a cheap pre-registered pilot. A fourth lap of the same
approach is not a plan; three sealed verdicts say so.

### Path C — the hybrid, made deliberate

Stress worlds score; generated worlds explore and narrate (world 801 already does).
Success means the labels are load-bearing: no generated world reaches a leaderboard
until it passes Path B's bars in full; no stress world is narrated without
join-awareness; every surface that shows both kinds says which is which. Path C is
the de facto present — its failure mode is exactly the implicitness that broke trust,
so it is only acceptable *explicit*.

---

## What each path costs

- **A** forecloses nothing technical. It costs the pitch: "alternate histories"
  becomes "prescribed histories," and the generative track moves from roadmap to
  research. It is buildable now, on machinery that is already proven.
- **B** is the only path to the original name. It is three strikes down, needs a
  genuinely new idea, and its bar (above) is deliberately hard — passing 4 and 5
  together means the generator has learned something no amount of resampling can fake.
- **C** is cheapest and is what we are already doing — which is the argument against
  it staying implicit. Explicit C is a fine waypoint; implicit C is how the owner
  ends up discovering the seams by asking about FOMC meetings.

## Recommendation

Adopt **A** as the product now. Keep **B** as gated research with this memo's bars
sealed as the promotion criteria, spent on only when a new idea clears the pilot
rule. Make **C** explicit everywhere both world types share a surface. Review the
PD-2 phase 2 draft ("SU generated worlds") against these definitions — as written it
is a Path-C document and should say so.

Under any path, one thing is non-negotiable: **the disclosure moves from the evidence
files into the player's face.** That is the trust repair, and it is owed to every
future player exactly as much as it was owed to the first one — who found the seams
by asking a good question.

# What happened next (2026-08-19)

*Appended four days after the memo above. The text above is unchanged — this
section says what the decisions actually were and how the forward bars read now.
Where the two disagree, this section is the later record, not a correction: the
memo was written to be checked, and this is the check.*

## Path A shipped

The recommendation was adopt A, gate B, make C explicit. **A was adopted and
built.** The app is finished and playable, and the engine underneath it was
released twice this week: ER-14 closed (`712f96d`) — inflation finally reaches
private markets through four mechanisms and a new infrastructure sleeve, and the
founding defect is literally inverted — and the successor worlds landed
(`9c15b2b`), giving the picker three playable declared-stress decades again,
including The Gulf Decade. Both went in on green gates. The detail is in
`docs/engine-realism-register.md`'s ER-14 close-out and in `NEXT-STEPS.md`.

## Path B was funded, and walked

B was not left on the shelf. It was funded four times in five days — the spine
pilot, the v2 campaign, stage 2 (the coupled monthly system), the reach fix, and
the three rulers — and every one of them ran under a seal taken before the
engine was fitted. **What it bought is real and it is not enough.**

**What passed for the first time.** The coupled engine cleared **nine of twelve**
sealed bars (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-18-stage2-results.md`). All four
persistence bars pass — the flaw that sank `hier-flow-v1` at G2 and the pilot
after it. **`T1` and `O1` both pass**, and `O1` had failed in the v2 campaign:
that is the reaction-function question, and it is the first time this platform
has had a generated world whose seasons turn the right way round. `P1` passes
with margin. And the reach fix took the agreement between a world's story and its
markets from **1.4 points over chance to 17.3**
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-18-stage2-reach-results.md`).

**What failed, with the mechanism named each time.** `P2` sits **above** its band
on dispersion stage 2 inherited rather than introduced. `A2` fails all three
conditions — and the reason is measured, not argued: the spine's conditioning
reached only **8.2%** of a decade, so the world's story and the world's markets
were very nearly independent. `R2` fails its join half at **4.13 pp against a
2.5 pp bound**. The new seam bar `S1` fails on **every engine in the lineage**: a
jump detector separates seams from ordinary historical months with 45–49 points
of advantage. And the arrow stage 2 was funded to build — growth driving
inflation — turned out to be **inert**: switching it off flips no verdict.

## The memo's forward bars, one by one

**Path A.** (1) *In-surface honesty* — **NOT MET.** Nothing in `app/` renders a
precedent line or a "how this world was made" panel; the disclosure still lives
in the evidence files, which is exactly what the memo called non-negotiable.
Proposed as WP-A. (2) *Severity binds, visibly* — **HALF MET.** Severity binds
and is measured, but the bands that shipped in `app-open-02` are allocation
bands; no measured severity band appears where the player picks the world.
(3) *The machinery can hurt you* — **MET.** ER-6 unblocked the commitment lever,
it is a playable control on every private sleeve, and coverage is the teaching
surface. (4) *The lesson is measured, not asserted* — **SUPERSEDED.** It is
defined over ranked sessions, and ranked play was parked by D-SP-7 on 2026-08-16;
it revives when ranked does. (5) *Nothing unscorable scores* — **MET, but
vacuously**, for the same reason: nothing scores at all while ranked is parked.

**Path B.** (1) *Calibrated pain* — **NOT MEASURED.** No bar in the stage-2 exam
tests drawdown understatement. (2) *Persistence* — **MET on this engine**, by
`D1`–`D4`, and that is the memo's hardest-won result. (3) *Judgeable — decade
tier ≥ 90%* — **NOT ADDRESSED.** (4) *It can hurt a book without being told to* —
**NOT MET.** `R1` passes, but on the **declared-premise** arm: severity that is
declared, not severity that emerges, which is precisely the distinction the bar
was written to draw. (5) *Internal causality* — **PARTIALLY MET, and this is the
new thing.** `T1` and `O1` both pass on a generated engine. The memo said the
stitched worlds can never pass this and nothing we had could; that is no longer
true. (6) *Still beats the benchmark* — **NOT TESTED.** No campaign since has run
a benchmark contest. **The conjunction the memo demanded has not been achieved,
and the spending rule it set still governs.**

## The honest residue

Three things a reader should carry, none of them comfortable.

**The hedge does not pay in these worlds.** Re-founded on 25,700 decades at a
batch size computed for 90% power, the inflation-hedge margin is **−1.54 pp**,
and **−3.14 pp** under the era rule
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-18-stage2-rulers-results.md`). The cause is
selection, not economics: the compiler draws from the worst third of the panel,
where bonds win. It is a precise verdict about this engine on this panel and it
is not a fact about commodities — but it also means every prior `A1` verdict, in
either direction, read a statistic whose sampling error was two to four times the
effect it measured. Nothing is re-graded. It is simply now known.

**The seams are still findable.** `S1` says so on all three engines, and the era
rule — which buys reach honestly, crossing only where the spine's own inflation
path crosses — makes every flesh bar slightly worse. Nobody has yet pulled the
one lever that would help without touching a declared tolerance: preferring
small-Δ joins.

**The engine is unpromoted.** The reach fix and the era rule are composed in
`scripts/` and have never touched `src/ah/gen/spine.py`. Nothing generated is
player-facing, no owner ruling has been taken on any of the stage-2 rulers, and
promotion is a separate release event that this campaign deliberately did not
request.
